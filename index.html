        Runway

        A research and scheduling tool for a creator, built on their past posts.

        280K+ followers on the channel it makes live posts for

        I built Runway so a creator could actually search their own back catalogue. It indexes 770+ past posts, drafts captions grounded in them, and schedules a post only after a person signs off.

        It’s used for live posts on a channel with 280K+ followers.

        Under the hood it keeps thousands of searchable image and text records, a cache of comparisons between them, and nearly 400 labeled creator preferences. I tested two ranking models and kept the stronger baseline.

        • Python
        • FastAPI
        • SQLAlchemy
        • SQLite
        • Next.js
        • TypeScript
        • NumPy

        Automation Supervisor

        The bot that keeps my other bots running, and restarts them when they fall over.

        29 workflows Under one supervisor, up from an 8-hour daily run window

        It keeps my workflows on schedule while I sleep, and it doesn’t take a run’s word for it: no fresh proof, no “done”.

        • Coordinates schedules so nothing runs twice or fights over the same resources
        • A run only counts as successful with fresh evidence that it finished
        • Recovers cleanly from crashes, outages, closed terminals, and machine restarts, tested against 70+ fault scenarios
